# Desks — a working mode is the desktop

**Status:** idea (parked 2026-10-12). Do not implement from this file.
Promote a slice into a freeze + plan + `CURRENT.md` **Now** when work starts.  
**As-built:** unassigned windows default to float. Zones are opt-in snaps.
Super+Tab is the app switcher (release Super to raise). Super+\` cycles
windows of the focused app. The browser, the terminal, and Workspaces each
own a tab strip inside one window. Each is its own process.  
**Locks this must not reopen:** Iced + sola-kit; River; bus + call plane;
multi-process restartability; CEF in `sola-browser`; Workspaces names
(`sola-workspaces`, window **Workspaces**, owner `workspaces`, tmux
`sola-ws` / `sws-`). See [`CURRENT.md`](../../CURRENT.md) **Locked models**.

A desk is a working mode: coding, mail, a game. The shell keeps a fixed list
of them down the left side. The selected desk shows one tiled plane. The
plane is a partition into groups. A group is a tab stack of panels. A panel
is one page, one terminal, one agent session, one game, or one tool.
Super+Tab steps between that plane and the desk's floating and fullscreen
panels. A new chord steps between desks.

Promoting this replaces zones as the way tiled work is arranged, and replaces
the app switcher as the primary way to move around. That is a windowing-model
change. Record it in **Locked models** in the same change as the freeze.
Until then, float + zones + per-app tab strips stay the product.

## One tab owner

The relationship "these belong together" is the desk. Today it lives in the
operator's head, smeared across a browser window, a terminal window, and the
Workspaces rail.

A group is heterogeneous. One group can hold a page, a shell, and an agent,
and shows one of them at a time. The strip on the group is the only tab
strip those panels have. The browser's vertical tab strip and the terminal's
sidebar are what this replaces.

Two kinds of structure stay inside the panel, because they are the anatomy
of one tool:

- A page keeps its omnibox, find bar, and devtools dock.
- Mail keeps its list and reader.

Peer documents become panels the desk can regroup: a second shell, a second
page, a second agent. Workspaces split leaves are peer documents. Devtools
are chrome of one page.

A group of one panel draws no strip.

## A copy is a set of views

Each desk has its own pages, shells, and agent sessions. The engines stay
the processes they already are.

- One browser process, one cookie jar, one vault.
- One terminal process.
- One agent host. Tmux sessions still live there.

A desk remembers which views it has and where they sit. Switching desks
shows a different set. Closing a desk drops its views. The engine stays up.
Hidden desks keep running, the same way a hidden app does now. A game keeps
running when its desk is not showing. Presentation is visibility, not a
process freeze.

The shell stores the desk list, the names, the splits, and a restore token
per panel: a URL, a working directory, a tmux session id. Engines keep their
own session persistence. The desk document points at those tokens.

Session chrome stays one per seat: menubar, flower, volume, Bluetooth,
notifications, launcher, shortcut overlay. A notification that belongs to a
panel switches to that desk and presents the panel.

Settings and the loupe are seat tools. Settings opens as a float layer.
The loupe follows the pointer on whichever desk is showing.

## The plane, and the two exceptions

Groups tile the desk rectangle and do not overlap. The product invariant is
the partition. The split algorithm (manual splits, dwindle, grid) can wait.

Gestures on the plane:

- Splitting divides the focused group into two groups.
- Dragging a tab out makes a new group.
- Dragging a tab onto a group joins that group.
- A new page or a new terminal joins the focused group.

Floating and fullscreen are other presentations of a panel. The panel still
belongs to the desk. Fullscreen remembers the group it came from, so leaving
it puts the panel back in that split. If it was the only panel in the group,
the group gives its rectangle back to its siblings and the split is restored
on the way out.

While a panel is fullscreen, the desk sidebar and the menubar step aside.
A game desk is that case with a single panel. Arcade's gamescope nest is
already a window. That window becomes the panel. Gamescope stays windowed
on the host (`-W` / `-H`).

**Recommended presentation, not yet decided:** the three layers are
exclusive. The plane is showing, or one float is showing, or one fullscreen
panel is showing. A float you are not on does not sit on top of the layout.
The other reading is Mac-like: floats always hover over the plane, and
Super+Tab only moves focus. See Forks.

Super+Tab does not walk tiles. The plane is one stop. Each float is a stop.
Each fullscreen panel is a stop. Inside the plane, Super+\` cycles the tabs
of the focused group, and arrows move between groups.

## The sidebar

Same idea as the browser's vertical strip, at desk scale. A desk is a
working mode, so the list stays short: a name, drag to reorder, and one
attention mark when an agent is waiting or mail is unread. No folders until
a real list needs them.

The strip is part of the plane. It is there whenever the layout is showing,
including on an empty new desk. An empty desk offers New page and New
terminal. The strip is absent while a fullscreen panel is the presented
layer.

Group tabs are a short horizontal strip on the tile. A second vertical
sidebar inside every tile spends width the tile does not have.

---

## The agent rail

Taken straight through, a project rail with its own tabs and splits is a
second desk living inside the desk.

**End state:** one agent session is one panel. Its status mark lives on the
group tab and rolls up to the desk row. Two agents side by side are two
groups on the plane. A project can be a desk you spawn, or a label on the
panel.

**Interim, so working modes can exist first:** a desk may host the rail as
one panel. Dissolving the rail is a later cut, and it is the cut that
matches the rest of this model.

Do not rename `sola-workspaces` to free the word "workspace". The call
plane, tmux prefix, and the window title stay as locked. The new object is
a **desk**. The sidebar title is Desks.

## Chords

Super is the command key.

| Chord | What it does |
|---|---|
| Super+Ctrl+Tab / Super+Ctrl+Shift+Tab | Next / previous desk |
| Super+Ctrl+1…9 | Jump to a desk |
| Super+Tab | Next presentation layer on this desk. Release Super to settle |
| Super+\` | Next panel in the focused group |
| ⌘T | New page in the focused group |
| ⌘⇧Enter | New terminal in the focused group |
| ⌘⇧→ / ⌘⇧↓ | Split the focused group right / down |

⌘T stays "new page here". The shell owns the strip that receives it. The
split chords are the ones Workspaces already uses. The launcher grows panel
kinds: it adds a page, a terminal, or an agent to the current desk.

Zones stop being how tiled work is arranged. A panel on the plane gets its
rectangle from the partition. A float stays freely positioned.

## Forks

Unsettled. Recommendations from the 2026-10-12 brainstorm. A freeze answers
these. Do not treat them as locked.

1. **Name.** Recommended: **Desk**. The agent app keeps Workspaces.
2. **Layers.** Recommended: exclusive. The plane, each float, and each
   fullscreen panel are separate presentations. The other reading keeps
   floats painted over the plane.
3. **Where a new panel lands.** Recommended: a new page or terminal joins
   the focused group. A split or a drag-out creates a group.
4. **Agent rail.** Recommended: one rail panel per desk is allowed as an
   interim. One session, one panel is the end state.
5. **Sidebar.** Recommended: plane chrome. Absent while fullscreen is the
   presented layer.
6. **Spawn chords.** Recommended: ⌘T new page, ⌘⇧Enter new terminal.

---

## Aim, once a freeze exists

Desks partition attention and layout. They are not separate logins,
profiles, or compositor sessions. One engine process serves every desk.
Peer documents are panels. Tool anatomy stays inside the panel. The group
strip is the only tab strip for those panels. Session chrome stays one per
seat.
