Privacy

Sola is a desktop environment that runs on your own computer. Its Calendar application can connect to your Google account. This page says what it does with that data. Calendar sends no analytics or telemetry.

Google Calendar

When you connect a Google account in Settings → Calendar, Sola asks Google for two permissions:

  • See and edit events on your calendars (calendar.events).
  • See the list of calendars you subscribe to (calendar.calendarlist.readonly).

It uses them to show your calendars and reminders in Calendar and in the menubar clock's panel, and to create, change, or delete an event when you do that in Calendar. Your computer talks to Google directly. Nothing passes through a server of ours, and we never see your calendars, your events, or your sign-in.

Where it is kept

On your computer, in your home directory. ~/.local/state/sola/calendar/ holds the calendars and events Sola last fetched, and your reminders. ~/.config/sola/state.yaml holds your connected accounts and how each calendar is shown, and ~/.config/sola/calendar/agenda.yaml the coming days for the clock's panel.

Sign-in tokens and passwords are encrypted on disk with a key in ~/.config/sola/key. Other programs running under your account can read these files, as with anything in your home directory.

Sharing

Sola does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. It sends your Google data to no one but Google.

Sola's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing it

In Settings → Calendar, open the account and choose Disconnect. That deletes the account, its sign-in tokens, its calendars, its events, and the reminders on them from your computer. If Calendar is closed, its copy goes when it next starts.

Disconnecting does not tell Google. To withdraw Sola's access there, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

iCloud and other calendars

An iCloud or CalDAV password is encrypted the same way and is used only to talk to that server. Calendar fetches a calendar URL directly from where it is hosted.

This site

sola.moonlight.pm sets no cookies and runs no script. Its fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for them. The server may keep ordinary request logs.
