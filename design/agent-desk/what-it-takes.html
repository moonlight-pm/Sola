05What it takes
Most of this is already half-built: Workspaces knows every agent's state from its hooks, the shell draws chips and cards from bus state, and River can draw colored borders. What is missing is one bus topic that carries agent state out of Workspaces, and a way to send a single key to a pane. Paths below are checked against master on 2026-10-13.

Risks
Key theftArmed mode holds digits from the app with focus. It is visible (cyan edge on chip and card), times out after ten seconds, and Esc lets go.
Answering the wrong promptThe pane can move on between the card and the key. The state and session check runs right before the send; if it fails the card leaves and nothing is sent.
Options from scrollbackClaude, Codex and Grok draw their menus differently and change between versions. When the options do not parse, the card shows only ↵ Open pane.
Repaint costA breathing border is compositor frames on an idle desk. Steady colors cost nothing between changes; ship those first.
Thin Codex cardsCodex panes on the app-server daemon get state from the rollout, not hooks, so their card may lack the tool input.
Not known yet
