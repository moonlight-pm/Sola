Where Sola differs

One person, one desk, all local. Agents are real CLIs in real terminals (tmux panes in Workspaces), and their state comes from their own hooks, not from reading the screen. An answer is a keystroke in that pane, so nothing happens out of sight and the pane's scrollback is the record.
