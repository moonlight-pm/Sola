From across the room · the same desk at 1:20

One window, one light

A Workspaces window carries the roll-up of its panes, the same order as the rail mark: waiting, then working, then done. A background tab that waits lights the window.

Focus is not a seam

Graphite keeps saying what has focus in the menubar. The seam only ever means an agent, so a lit edge is never a question of which window you are typing into.

Brightness budget

Working peaks at 62% so a waiting zone always outshines it. With every zone at work the desk is a dim cyan grid, and one amber edge still finds you.

Floats and fullscreen

A floating window keeps its titlebar and shadow and gets no seam; its agent still shows in the strip. River draws no borders on a fullscreen window.

No agent, no light

Browser, Mail and the rest stay bare. Shells and idle panes never light. If nothing is running the desk looks exactly like Graphite.

Reduced motion

Breath holds still at 45%, the waiting pulse and the done flash are skipped. Color and steadiness still carry the state.
