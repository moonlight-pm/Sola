Title
The rail label and "is waiting". The timer counts from the moment the hook said so.
Source
Project, agent, and what kind of ask: run a command, edit a file, answer a question.
Context
The three lines above the prompt, read from the pane. Mono, in the field well, clipped, never wrapped.
Ask
One line from the hook payload: the tool and its input. A command is mono in cyan, because it is what will run.
Keys
The agent's own options, numbered as the pane numbers them. ↵ always opens the pane. Armed, the keys light cyan: they are live.
Size
340 wide like every desk card, 12px from the right edge. Height follows the content; three lines of context at most.

Keys only while armed

Super+A arms; while a card is armed the shell holds 1–9, Tab, ↵ and Esc, and lets them go on answer, on Esc, or after ten seconds without a key. Unarmed, every key belongs to the app with focus.

One question, one key

There is no answer-all and no "always allow" from the desk. An option that writes a standing rule, like Codex's "this session", says so in its own words and is still one press for one agent.

Words go to the pane

"No, and tell Claude" needs typing, so it opens the pane with the cursor in Claude's prompt. Clicking the card body does the same. A click on a key answers without moving focus.
