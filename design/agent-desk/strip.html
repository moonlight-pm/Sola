The card drops 6px below the bar under its chip, like a menu, and follows the pane while the pointer rests. Click opens the pane: Workspaces comes forward on that tab.

Order

Waiting first, longest wait leftmost. Then working, oldest first. Done last, newest first. A chip moves only when its state changes, never while you read.

Names

The chip uses the rail label: the worktree slug or the tab's title. When two live agents share a label, the project goes in front in caps: SOLA master, EXIT master.

What it never shows

Idle panes, plain shells, and the pane you are looking at. The strip is a list of who might need you, not a process list.
