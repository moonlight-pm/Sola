What changes from Graphite
Numbers become readouts. Clock, counts, and drop-panel values are dots on the 3px LED grid. Cells are fixed, so the bar never reflows.
Labels are engraved. Iosevka Fixed Extended, caps, tracked, muted. Menu titles and sentences stay SF Pro.
Shell surfaces go neutral black. #0a0a0b and #121314 with hairline seams. Apps keep Graphite's blue graphite.
Color by function. Network gives up cyan. The spectrum is one phosphor with a white peak dot. Meters print orange past 75%, red past 90%.
Gold is the system's alone. Waiting moves from amber to orange and keeps its diamond.
Two new modules. Capture lamps (MIC, CAM) and an agent count beside the meters.
Super+K is a keycap map. Hold Super and the keys that do something light.
