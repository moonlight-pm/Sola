The front panel
One 28px bar, read left to right like a rack unit. Words on the left, instruments on the right. Every module has a fixed footprint, an engraved label where a glyph would not do, and lights only in the function key's colors. Nothing reflows when a number changes.
Anatomy · 1:1
SystemThe flower, 14px, gold. The only warm point in the bar and its only brand.
Focused appBold SF Pro, as today. Zoned windows have no titles, so this word says what has focus.
MenusSF Pro 13. Words stay type. An open menu takes an 18% ink fill.
ExtrasMail and missed notices: the glyph and a fixed readout. Cyan when something waits for you, dark when not.
LampsBT, MIC, CAM. Labels stay dark until their lamp lights. MIC and CAM are red tallies while any app holds the device. New.
AgentsRing: working. Diamond: waiting. One cell each, counted from the Workspaces rail. New.
OutThe 12-band spectrum in one phosphor, green. A white dot holds each band's recent peak.
MetersCPU, GPU, MEM, RX, TX: 12 × 5 LEDs, white at rest. A column prints orange past 75%, red past 90%.
ClockA 17 × 5 readout, exactly the meters' height. The day and date are engraved beside it.
States · 1:1
IdleNothing needs you. Readouts sit dark, meters run white, and the clock is the brightest thing on the right.
WarmA release build. CPU columns past 75% print orange, past 90% red. The label does not change; the meter says it.
For youTwelve unread, three missed notices, one agent waiting. Cyan is for you; orange asks for a look.
LiveA Slack huddle in Browser holds the mic and camera. Two red tallies stay lit, steady, until it lets go.
WhisperScreenshot copied. A whisper is a sentence, so it stays SF Pro in the middle of the bar for a few seconds.
CPU drop panel · 1:1
Value5×7 on a 4 / 5 grid in a recessed window. Four cells, sized for 100%, so 83% keeps a dark cell and never shifts. Tinted by the same rule as the meter.
HistoryThe last 60 samples, one column each, 8 rows tall. It fills the card exactly: 60 × 5 px.
Per threadOne column per thread, siblings side by side, a dark column between the 16 cores. No numbers: the shape is the point.
Lists stay typeProcess names in SF Pro, figures in Iosevka. Dots are for the number you came for; type is for the rest.
One grid per displayThe bar is 2 / 3. A drop panel is 4 / 5. Nothing else lights.
