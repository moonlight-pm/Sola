Rules
Dots for the number you came for.The clock, a count, a time, a panel's value. Words and everything else stay type.
Readouts have fixed cells.A count of 1 and a count of 12 take the same space. Nothing in the bar reflows.
Unlit dots stay faintly visible.The field shows a readout's capacity, and an empty readout reads as zero.
One grid per display.The bar is 2 / 3, a drop panel 4 / 5. The mark's 8 / 12 is for the cover and the installer only.
One color, one meaning.Four keys and two lamps. Lamps are steady: nothing in chrome blinks.
A label only where a glyph won't do.Mail, bell, and the spectrum need none. Meters and lamps get one, engraved.
