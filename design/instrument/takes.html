Risks
GPU idle
Every readout is a cached image rebuilt on change. Lamps are steady, glows are baked or skipped, nothing blinks. The spectrum keeps its existing self-wake only while live (PERFORMANCE.md).
Legibility at 1:1
3×5 on 2 px dots is small on the 5120 × 2160 panel. Keep 3×5 to digits and the colon in the bar; try it on the desk before the rest.
Gold and orange
#ffb800 and #ff8a3d are about 20° apart. If they blur on the real panel, waiting keeps its diamond and drops the hue to cyan outline.
Costume
Dots spreading is the failure mode. "Dots for the number you came for" is the guard; a dot marquee or dot menu titles break it.
