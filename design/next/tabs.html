NEXT · 06One tab list
The master bar takes over every app's tabs. The browser's strip, the Projects rail, and the terminal's tabs go away; each page, shell, and agent session is a tab in the bar. A zone holds tabs of any kind and shows one, and its titlebar is the titlebar of what it shows. The workspace switcher is back in the top row, and the bar lists only the workspace showing there.

Anatomy · the bar at 1:1
One workspaceThe bar heads with the workspace the top row says is showing, and lists only its tabs. Switching workspace swaps the bar with the desk.
Zones hold anythingCenter has two pages and two shells. Order is yours: drag rows to arrange, across kinds.
Agents are tabsEach agent session is a row in a zone, with its mark: kit-foundation working, menubar-pass waiting, mail-focus done. The Projects rail is gone.
New tabsThe + on a zone header opens a page, a shell, or an agent there; ⌘T and ⌘⇧Enter add to the focused zone.
Empty zonesA zone with no tabs keeps its header as a drop target and gives its width to the others.
The stack countA titlebar shows how many tabs its zone holds (4), so you know without looking left.
