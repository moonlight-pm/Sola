SOLA · FOUNDATION · 2026-10-14 · AFTER FOUNDATION V1 AND FOUR EXPLORATIONS
Apps carry their own menus. Windows stand apart. The top of the desk belongs to the islands.
Today's bar holds the focused app's menus across the top of a 5120 px output, far from the window they act on, and that is why the bar exists at all. Next puts each app's menus in its own titlebar, gives every window one, and spaces windows one gap apart. With nothing left to hold, the bar goes away, and the top row of the gap grid is free for islands.

Three moves
01 · Menus on the window

Menus move onto the app

The menus each app already publishes — Workspaces' Project and Edit, Mail's Mailbox and Message — sit in that app's titlebar, after its name. The name is the app menu, as it is in the bar today, and the shell still adds Window last.

A menu acts on the window it hangs from, wherever that window is on 5120 px.

01 · Menus on the window

Every window wears a titlebar

Tiles get the 38 px titlebar the kit gives floats today: close, name, menus, title, and a zone glyph. Focus is the window: a brighter titlebar, a lit rim, a red close dot.

Apps that are not ours get a titlebar from sola-river when they accept server-side decorations, and keep their own when they do not.

02 · Gaps · 03 · The top row

Gaps, and the top is islands

One 10 px gap between windows, around the output, and above and below 28 px islands, so tiles start at 48. The islands are the grid's top row: the flower lines up with the first tile, the clock with the last.

No band behind them. The ground in the gaps is the ground under the islands.
