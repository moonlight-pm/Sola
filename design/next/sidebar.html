NEXT · 05The master sidebar
One vertical bar down the left owns where everything is. Each workspace is a main row; under it sit its three zones and a floating zone, and under each zone the windows it holds. A zone shows one window at a time, so the bar is how you reach the others. The top row keeps the flower, the live bubble, and the instruments; the bar replaces board 04's workspace bubble. Projects is the agent app, as on 04.

Anatomy · the bar at 1:1
Workspace rowNumber, name, a chevron. The showing workspace is open; the others fold to one row with the icons of what they hold and their marks (Mail's cyan dot: unread).
Zone headerLeft, Center, Right, Floating, each with the zone glyph from the titlebars and a count. A header is a drop target.
Window rowApp icon and window title. The window a zone is showing is lifted a step; the focused one is a pill. The rest are in tertiary ink: open, but behind.
Marks ride alongProjects' working ring sits on its row, so an agent is visible even when its window is behind another in the zone.
The titlebar says there is moreA stack count beside the zone glyph (2) tells you the zone holds another window, without opening the bar.
Width240 px, plus one gap. On 5120 that is 4.9% of the output; on 2560 it is 9.8%.
