Real and invented
Real: names, needs, versions, hashes, programs, services, sizes, overlays and their commits, the newest release's pins for the 15 Sola packages, and upstream versions from the audit.
Invented, and tagged sample on the page: the unpushed sola-browser, updates to openssl, curl and git, all advisories, and ffmpeg and gimp on the origin.
Assumed: outside those, the newest release leaves every package as it is. I have not read what it would change.
What the app cannot read yet
Is it pushed? Oath records repo, commit, branch and dirty, but not whether origin has the commit. The app has to ask git.
What would an update change? That is the preview from oath update, whose output this comp has not seen.
What is on the origin but not here? It needs a listing of the store, and I do not know that one exists.
Security. The advisory file has id, url and summary, not severity or a fixed version, and a local build has no file at all.
Upstream. The audit knows, but its report stays on buccina. Oath has been asked to publish it.
Sizes. Tonight's read used du. They are not in the catalog.
