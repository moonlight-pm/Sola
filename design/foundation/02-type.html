Rules
One bold word in a titlebarThe app's name, Semibold, as its first menu. Everything else in the row is regular. Caps are labelsSection and zone labels: 10 px Medium, uppercase, tracked 1 px, tertiary ink. Never a sentence. Figures in the top rowThe clock and counts are chrome type at 13, not mono, as a menu bar clock is. The meters say their numbers as fixed LED grids, so they never reflow. Code is monoCommands, paths, hex values and the terminal. Prose and names stay in the UI face. The bus sizesThe theme seeds text-caption 11, text-body 12, text-body-lg 13, text-heading 16 and text-display 20, but nothing reads them; the kit's sizes are constants. See 11.
