Surfaces
What opens over the desk, drawn as the code draws it today on the grey base. The launcher and the switcher sit in the middle of the output. Notices stack at the top right. The agent card and every panel hang from their island. Menus open from the flower, from the tab bar's head, and from a window's titlebar.
Launcher · Super+Space · 1:1

A 560 px kit modal in the middle of the output, over a 60% black backdrop. Corners 14, a 1 px bevel, shadow 38% y 8 blur 28. The query is 16 px in an inset well, pad 12, then a divider. Rows are a 20 px icon and a 14 px label, gap 10, pad 8 × 12, in a 360 px list inset 6. The selected row is the hover grey at radius 5, not the accent.

Switcher · Super+Tab · 1:1

One icon per app with an open window, on every workspace, most recent first; it opens on the second. A 72 px icon in a 96 px cell, 6 apart. The selected cell gets a white 18% plate at radius 10 and the only caption (13 px). Unread mail and unseen notices show as the accent count mark at the cell's corner. The backplate is the switcher token (raised at 90%), a 1 px white 10% edge, radius 14, pad 14 × 22, and no shadow.

Notices and the missed pile · 1:1

Notices are 340 × 76 popover cards, 8 apart, at most 3 live, each held 6 s. They rest 12 px from the right edge and 6 px under the top row (y 44), and drop in from 14 px higher over 180 ms; they leave in 140 ms. Missed ones go to the pile: the bell island shows the count in the accent until you open it, and the pile opens as a 320 px panel under it, grouped by app.

The agent card · 1:1

Clicking an agent island opens its card under the island's left edge: a 440 px popover, pad 12. The head repeats the island (mark, name in medium, what it wants at 55%) with the project and age at the right. The body is mono when the agent wants to run a command, at most 220 px tall. Choices fill the width, the first primary and the rest secondary; a done session or a free answer gets a reply field. Show (small ghost) brings up the session's window.

Menus · 1:1

The flower's menu and a workspace's menu are the shell's: kit popover chrome (radius 7, pad 4), 13 px rows padded 3 × 10 with a 14 px check slot, shortcuts 12 px at 55%, a grey hover (the hover grey at 75%, radius 5). A window's menu is the kit's own sheet, drawn inside the window: radius 12, 26 px rows, radius 7, and an accent hover at 14%. Two menu styles are live today.

Toast · the center of the top row · 1:1

A toast is an island in the true center of the row, 13 px chrome type. An error stays 5 s; "Opening …" stays up to 20 s, until the app's window appears.

Panels hang from their island · CPU · 1:1

Each instrument opens a 324 px popover whose left edge is under its label, its top on the row's lower edge (y 38), kept a gap from the output's right edge; the island lifts while it shows. It draws the island's LEDs larger, 4 px dots on a 5 px pitch, whole (a dot lights once its column reaches its middle; no dither): the value in large type (SF Pro Text medium, 28 px), tinted like the meter; the last 60 seconds as columns, each tinted by its own sample; per-thread load as a column per thread, a gap between threads and two between cores (side by side, a pair read as one bar); top processes with a 16-cell LED bar; memory and VRAM as a 60-cell row (used lit, cache at 40%, free dark). Captions are 10 px caps, figures Iosevka. Every color is the theme's: ink at rest, warning past 75%, danger past 90%; RX in the accent, TX in success. Design: Instrument 07.
