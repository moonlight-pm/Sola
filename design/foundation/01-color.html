The picture sets the colors · Settings → Appearance
Colors are modalThe shell samples the picture at 96 px and counts its colors in narrow OKLCH cells (10° of hue, 0.02 chroma, 0.1 lightness), scored by share and chroma. The most common colors win, not an average. Up to four schemesOne per major hue, at first 90° apart, closing to no less than 30°. Each is named by its hue: Rose, Ember, Amber, Gold, Olive, Green, Teal, Sky, Blue, Violet, Magenta, or Grey. The accentAt least 60° from the scheme's tint, at lightness 0.82 (stepping down to 0.68 to stay in gamut), chroma 0.06 to 0.14. The surfacesKeep their grey's lightness and take the tint at chroma up to 0.08 × strength, thinner below lightness 0.18. Text takes 30% of the tint. Status hues are untouched. Strength0 to 100%, 60 by default. OrderThe greys, then the scheme, then your colors on top. Your colorsWindow sets bg-primary; Panels sets bg-secondary and the switcher at 90%; Highlight sets highlight, and selection takes its hue at its own lightness; Accent sets accent, text-accent and accent-dim at 10%. A change made on a scheme saves a new look beside the schemes, up to six.
