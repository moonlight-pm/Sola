The window
Every kit window draws the same frame: a 38 px titlebar that carries the app's own menus, 12 px corners, and a 1 px rim. Focus is the window itself: a brighter bar, a red close dot, the highlight on the rim. Other apps' tiles get the same corners and rim from sola-river; there is no global menubar.

The Window menu, as the kit publishes it · 1:1
Every kit app gets this menu unless it publishes its own. Hide, Cycle Windows, Float, Left, Right, Full and Cinema act. Top, Bottom and the Middle zones are left from the three-column desk: the shell logs "no such zone on the Full / halves layout" and does nothing (dimmed here; see 11).
Narrow windows · 1:1
The title goes first: cut with an ellipsis, then left out under 32 px of room. Then menus that do not fit are left off from the right, the Window menu with them. There is no fold into "···".
Corners clear to the ground · 4×
Other apps' windows · 1:2
An app that draws no Sola frame (Steam, any foreign app) gets no titlebar. Tiled, sola-river rounds it with four decorations above the window — the corners painted from the ground or the picture under them, and the rim along the edge — so it sits in the grid like a kit tile. Floating, it stays square with River's 1 px border in the rim colors, outside the content.

The numbers
Titlebar38 tall (window_frame::HEIGHT), a 1 px line of black at 50% under it. Focused: the raised fill. Unfocused: halfway from the face to raised; ink at 55%, the title at 38%. Corners12 (window_frame::RADIUS), tiled and floating alike. The corners outside the radius are cleared, so the ground shows: black, or the picture. Rim1 px, opaque. Focused focus_rim(highlight); rest lift_in_hue(face, 0.07). Opaque because a translucent rim let the picture brighten every edge. Close#ff5f57, hover #ff736b; unfocused white at 12% (20% on hover). No yellow or green lights. DropdownInside the window (dropdown.rs): pad 5, rows 26, row radius 7, sheet radius 12, min 200, margin 4. ShadowFloats only, drawn by sola-river: 28%, y 3, blur 11, corner 12. Tiles cast none. ForeignNo server-side titlebar. Tiles: corners and rim from client/corners.rs. Floats: River's square 1 px border (client/manage.rs). BareCinema: content only, square, opaque, no titlebar.
