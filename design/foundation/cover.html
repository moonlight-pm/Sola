Sola
Instruments in graphite.
A desktop for one person who keeps terminals, agents, mail, and a browser open at once on a 5120-wide desk. Chrome is quiet graphite and reads at a glance, like a panel of instruments at night. One cyan signal marks what is focused, primary, or working. The only warm things are the mark and the cursor.
One signal
Cyan marks focus, the one primary action, and live work. Never a region, a heading, or decoration.
The ladder
Depth is a step up the graphite ladder. Edges are opaque white mixes at 7% and 12%.
Only floats cast
Popovers, cards, the launcher, and floating windows have shadows. Docked chrome is flat.
Shape first
Working is a ring, waiting a diamond, done a check. A column reads without color.
Pixel meters
Chrome meters are 2px LEDs. Numbers live in the dropdown.
Zones, no titles
Zoned windows meet the zone edge bare. Floating windows wear the titlebar and round all four corners.
