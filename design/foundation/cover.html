Sola

Windows on the ground, islands above them.

A desktop for one person who keeps terminals, agents, mail and a browser open at once. Every window carries its own menus and sits one gap from its neighbors. The top row is a handful of islands, each the thing you click. Under everything is the ground: black, or a picture whose colors the theme takes on. This sheet is what is real — the kit, the shell and sola-river as built.

Menus ride the window

A 38 px titlebar on every kit window: close, the app's name in bold, its menus, the title. There is no global menubar.

One gap

10 px at the edges, between windows, above and below the islands. Tiles start at y 48.

Islands, not a bar

28 px pills on the ground: the flower, agents, a toast, the instruments, the clock.

One tab list

The tab bar lists the showing workspace's windows by zone. Its head switches workspaces.

The picture sets the colors

Optionally. The theme takes the picture's modal colors as schemes; your own colors sit on top.

Focus is a rim

The focused window's rim is the highlight a step lighter; its close dot is red. The rest dim to 55% and stay put.

Shape first

Working is a ring, waiting a diamond, done a check. A row reads without color.

Only floats cast

Tiles, the tab bar and the islands are flat with rims. Floats, popovers and the launcher have shadows.

Since Graphite v1 (2026-10-12)
  • The base is grey. The cool blue-grey ladder is retired; saved slate values map to greys on load.
  • Menus moved onto the windows. The 28 px global menubar and its bold app name are gone.
  • Every window has a titlebar. Tiles are no longer bare and flush; they sit a gap apart with 12 px corners.
  • The top row became islands. No band; the focused app's name left the row.
  • Rails are gone. Projects, Browser, Terminal, Paint and Design are a window per document, listed in the tab bar.
  • Zones are Full, Left, Right, Floating. Full and the halves take turns on screen.
  • Workspaces are desks. Named, each with its own windows and browser profile.
  • The ground can be a picture. The theme can follow it; a highlight color and your own looks joined the palette.
  • The flower is gold. Old proposal P1, done.
  • The desk fades when idle. The picture rises over it; the flower and asking agents stay in sight.
