Settings and the picture
Every Settings page is one column of sections: a caption, a group of rows on one raised surface, a caption under it. A row that opens shows its editor in place, under it, so the page is only as tall as what it holds. Appearance is where the background picture, the colors it gives, your own looks and the idle fade are set.
Settings → Appearance · canto's theme (Rose, from the Cosmic Cliffs) · the Accent picker open · 1:1
1
The windowA kit window: Settings, Edit, Window in its titlebar; the title would repeat the app's name, so it is left off. The sidebar is the kit's, 220 wide on the chrome surface; the page is the rest, padded 24, its heading 22.

ColorsAdapt on or off, Strength (default 60%), then the picture's schemes as small windows: the raised bar, the accent pill, and beside the name the picture's color it came from. The one in use is ringed in the accent. Your looks follow them.

5
IdleFade the windows when idle (on), Start after 1–60 min (5), Fade over 1–120 min (30).

Anatomy · the grey base · 1:1
PageOne column, at most 600 wide, sections 28 apart; it scrolls under the heading. SectionA caption head (11 px, muted, 26 tall) with an action at its right — a small secondary button: "+ Add", "Remove Custom". The group 4 under it; a muted caption under that. GroupRaised, lit 4% at its top (card_fill), a 7% edge, radius 10, an inset of 4. A 1 px rule (white 6%) between rows, 10 in from each side. Settingform_row: 32 tall, the label at full ink filling the row, its control at the right. Padded 2 × 10 / 8. Opening rowA 20 px leading mark, the title over a muted subtitle, a note, a chevron at 45% that turns down while open. Hover lifts 3.5%, pressed 6%, open 5%; radius 7. EditorUnder its row in the same group, padded 8 / 10 / 12. Its footer: small buttons left (Save, Discard) and right (Remove). No detail pane. Sliders200 wide with a 56 px value. They are iced's own style; the kit has no slider of its own yet.
The picture
Set itPaint: File → Set as Background. Or solactl background, which can also clear it. Sola keeps its own copy in ~/.config/sola/background/. DrawnBy sola-river, at the bottom of everything, filling the output and cropped about its center. With none, the ground is black. CornersKit windows, the tab bar and the top row clear their corners, so the picture shows there. Other apps' tiles get corners painted from it, and the rim over them. ColorsWith Adapt on, the shell samples it at 96 px and finds its modal colors: up to four schemes, each a tint for the greys and an accent at least 60° away. Status colors stay. See 01.

A tile's corner at 6×: outside the 12 px radius the window is clear and the picture shows; the 1 px rim is opaque, a step lighter than the face in its hue.

The idle fade · 1:4
WhenAfter Start after with no input (ext_idle_notify, so an app that keeps the screen awake holds it off). Never while a window is fullscreen. Howsola-river lays the picture (or black) over everything as shell surfaces with no input region, and raises their opacity with wp_alpha_modifier_v1: linear over Fade over, a step at most once a second, no repaint. KeptThe piece over the flower and the agent islands (Topic::IdleKeep) is never covered, so they stay in full sight: what might want an answer. BackAny input brings the windows back over a second, easing out.
