The tab bar and workspaces
One tab list for the whole desk. The tab bar sits in the grid's left column and lists the showing workspace's windows by zone; its head is the workspace switcher. Apps open a window per document, so every page, tab, image and project is a row here.

Workspaces · 1:6
A workspaceIts own desk: its own windows in their zones, its own browser profile. Named; a fresh desk starts with Main. Hidden ones keep running. Show oneSuper+1…9, Super+Ctrl+Tab and Super+Ctrl+Shift+Tab, or the head's menu. Its last focused window takes focus. Send a windowSuper+Ctrl+1…9 sends the focused window there; the showing workspace keeps focus on what is left. Super+TabThe switcher lists apps across every workspace. AttentionAnother workspace's state rolls up beside the head's name: a diamond where a tab waits on a decision, outranking a dot for unread mail.

Where a window goes
NewJoins the focused zone, in front. The first window on an empty workspace goes to Full. ReturningA window that comes back (an app restart, a re-exec after install) gets its saved place: its place waits 30 s, the whole saved layout two minutes at session start. MovingDrag a row to reorder it or onto another zone. ⌘ Pad 4 / 6 / 0 / * send the focused window to Left, Right, Full or Float. ShowingA click shows the window in its zone and focuses it. The one it covers stays in the list, behind. ClosingHover a row and its red stoplight closes that window.
The numbers
BarA kit island 240 wide, at x 10 and y 48, a gap from the bottom. The raised fill, a 1 px rim 12% lighter in its hue, 12 px corners cleared to the ground. Head28 tall, 10 px in. The workspace's name in semibold 13 and a 12 px chevron at 55%. Hover white 7%, open 14%, corners 7. Workspace menu220 wide (the head's width), hanging 2 px under it. 24 px rows: each workspace with Super+N and the showing one checked; New Workspace, Rename…; Move Left, Move Right; Delete. ZonesFull, Left, Right, Floating, in that order: 10 px caps. A zone with no windows keeps its label. Rows32 tall, 6 × 10 pad, 13 px, a 16 px leading slot, 4 px corners. Behind: ink at 70%. Shown: lifted 5% in its hue, ink still 70%. Bright: full ink, medium, no lift. Focused: the highlight, medium. Hover: lifted 7%, full ink, the 12 px stoplight. MarksA working or waiting tab shows its mark in the icon's slot, so the title does not move. Idle shows the app's icon.
