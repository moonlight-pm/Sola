Space, shape, edge, depth
Space steps by four, and the desk by one 10 px gap. Corners grow with the size of the thing: 4 for a row, 7 for a control, 12 for a window, a full round for an island. Edges are opaque mixes. Only floats, popovers and the launcher cast a shadow; everything docked is flat and rimmed.

Iced blends in linear light, so a translucent white line reads about three times brighter than the same CSS. The kit bakes each edge as an opaque mix on the surface it sits on (style.rs mix_white); on a tinted scheme, rims lift in the surface's own hue instead.
