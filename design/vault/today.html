What is wrong with it
No frame of its own. A modal card (14 px, the deep shadow) dropped over the page, not a sheet hanging from the key button. Every state picks its own width, so the panel jumps when you go from the list to an item or a form.
Everything shouts at once. Search, five chips, a host line, "Autofill", two filled Fill buttons, "All items", an account row, Log out, Refresh, Close. The thing you came for — fill this site — is one of a dozen.
Cyan everywhere. Every suggestion carries a filled primary, and the passkey badge is cyan too. Graphite keeps the accent to field focus and the one primary.
Words where shapes would do. "passkey" and "code" badges, the code's seconds as "· 18s" text, "For github.com" under the search, "Sign in to For github.com".
The record is a stack of labels. Label over value, no rows, no copy on each field, the password with no reveal, the passkey a lone badge, Fill twice (head and authenticator).
Lists in fixed boxes. 220, 360, 400, 420 px, with the empty space left over (the passkey pick) or the list clipped under the footer.
Housekeeping in the way. Account, Log out, Refresh and Close sit at the foot of the list every time; Escape and a click away already close it.
No keyboard path. Opening the panel does not put you anywhere: no selected row, no Enter to fill, no arrows.
