The parts
The head says where you are. The site, with its own mark, while the page has one; "Vault" otherwise; the item inside a record. New (+) and ··· are the only buttons up there.
A plate names the item. Its first letter on its enamel, hashed from the name — the way Sola names profiles. Cards, identities and notes take a grey plate with their glyph.
Marks, not badges. A fingerprint for a passkey, a clock for a one-time code, in tertiary ink after the name.
One way to fill. The chosen row reads Fill ↵; a click on any row of this site fills it, a click on its › opens it. No row carries a filled button.
The list grows, then scrolls. The sheet takes its content's height up to 560, and the list scrolls inside with a fade at its foot. No box of fixed height.
The foot is a status line. When it last synced and who is signed in. A failed sync turns its mark into the warning diamond and puts a Retry at the top.
