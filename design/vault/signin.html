Unlock asks for one thing. The remembered account is the head, so the form is just the master password. Email shows only for another account.
Codes in boxes. Six boxes for an authenticator code (paste fills them all); one field for the emailed code, which has letters.
The site is the head. A passkey request names the site with its mark and the action; rows are the accounts. ↵ signs in, Esc cancels and hands the page its error, so it can offer another way.
Save adds first. When this site already has a login without a passkey, adding to it is the chosen row; a new login is the secondary choice.
