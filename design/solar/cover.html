Solar
Sola keeps the sun's hours.
Graphite is a desk at night. Solar gives it the rest of the day: four lights — dawn, day, dusk, night — on one structure, with forty-minute fades around sunrise and sunset. By day things cast shadows; by night they emit light. The flower is the one thing the sun never changes.

What changes from Graphite

  1. 1Graphite becomes Night, one of four lights. Nothing in it changes.
  2. 2Sola gets a light theme (Day) and two twilights between it and Night.
  3. 3Depth follows the light: shadows by day, rim light and glow by night.
  4. 4The menubar's bottom edge is a horizon. The sun travels it from sunrise to sunset.
  5. 5The clock dropdown shows sunrise, sunset, day length, and the next change of light.
  6. 6Surfaces fade in OKLCH. Ink never fades: it flips once, where the other side reads better.
