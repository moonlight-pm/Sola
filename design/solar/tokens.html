Tokens and fades
Each light is a full set of the bus palette's tokens, so a component reads one name and the hour supplies the value. Contrast is checked per light on the raised surface. Between lights, surfaces fade and ink flips.

Surface lightness across the fade
1Surfaces fade over forty minutes in OKLCH. Hue and chroma move evenly. When a fade crosses from light to dark, lightness eases on a steep curve so the desk spends about six minutes in mid-gray (L 0.40–0.75), not eighteen.
2Ink is chosen, not blended. Text takes whichever light reads better on the blended surface, so it flips once, at the crossover, instead of passing through gray.
3One theme a minute. Forty emits per change, about 160 a day. Each is a full redraw of every open window, once.
4Sol is in no light. The flower, the terminal cursor, and the sun on the line read one constant.
