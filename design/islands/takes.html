engineering, or outside Sola

The morph

The notify overlay takes one live Frame at the card's largest size plus shadow pad, centered over the island, and stays above the bar. It draws the island exactly as the bar does, so the handoff has no seam; then iced springs the shape inside the surface with Animation and a custom lilt easing. One swapchain size per morph, frames only for its ≤ 350 ms.

crates/sola-shell/src/zoning.rscrates/sola-shell/src/notify/view.rsiced_core 0.14 animation.rs (lilt Easing::Custom)

Clicks through the morph's pad

Transparent pixels in that Frame still take the pointer. The patched iced_winit already sets the opaque region from the window fill; it would also need to set an input region to the shape, or the pad blocks a strip of the Browser for the card's life.

Blur

Not in River 0.4.5: neither /opt/sola/bin/river nor libwlroots-0.20 carries ext-background-effect-v1, and wlroots' scene renderer has no blur pass. It needs a River patch with a blur pass (the sway fork SwayFX gets one from SceneFX) or an upstream change. Until then the fill is opaque.

nix/patches/ (River + wlroots patches live here)
Risks

GPU idle stays where it is

River GLES scans out ARGB only with a full opaque region, so tiled windows must look opaque (PERFORMANCE.md; architecture.md, "Tiled kit opaque-region"). Islands-only keeps every tile square and opaque. Gapped would not.

Never resize per frame

Overlays are parked 2 × 2 off-output and shown through a live Frame. The morph sets its Frame once and animates inside; a Frame per tick would reallocate the swapchain every frame.

Presents only while moving

Iced presents every window in the process after any message. Morph frames are requested only during the spring; a waiting card, a working ring, and a ticking timer redraw once a second at most.

Flapping agents

An agent that toggles between working and waiting would morph all day. The whisper holds at least 1.5 s; the card opens only for waiting, and only once per question.

The top is far on a 2160 desk

The switcher at the top center needs a desk test before it replaces the centered strip. The launcher at the top left has the same question at 5120 wide.

Whole pixels

The dogfood desk is 1×. Island y, heights, and the rim must land on whole pixels or the 1 px rim smears into two rows.
