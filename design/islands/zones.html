ISLANDS · THE ZONE QUESTIONFlush, gapped, or islands only
Floating chrome raises a fair question: should the tiles float too? Three treatments of the same 5120 × 2160 desk, each as a quarter of the desk at 1:4 and as two 1:1 crops: where the desk meets the output's corner, and where Workspaces meets Browser.
Recommendation

Islands only. Keep the zones flush.

  1. A corner should mean "this floats". Graphite already says only floats cast shadows. With square tiles, rounded corners and shadows mark the same set of things: shell surfaces and floating windows.
  2. Gaps ask every app for corners. River 0.4.5 does not round or clip a client's buffer, so each kit app would clip its own 12 px corners and give up its full opaque region. Xwayland windows and other foreign apps would stay square.
  3. The band is the cheap half. It is one opaque surface 12 px taller than today's bar, and the GPU idle work on tiled windows stays as it is.
  4. Gapped can come back as a preset. Omarchy 4 ships rounded and sharp as one control. If Sola wants that, it is zoning geometry plus River work, not a v1 decision.
