Rules

Size springs, content fades

Text never scales. Each state's content is laid out at its final size and revealed by the island's edge as it grows.

Grow may overshoot; shrink never

The card and the open card overshoot 4% and 2%. Closing is critically damped, so the pill lands on the band without a bounce.

Leave first, arrive late

Outgoing content is gone in 90 ms. Incoming content fades in over 140 ms once the shell is 40% through its spring.

350 ms is the ceiling

The longest step is the card, 320 ms. A morph that needs longer is the wrong surface.

Reduced motion

No springs. The island fades out in 80 ms, takes its new size, and fades in over 120 ms. The toggle above shows it.

Frames only while moving

Iced presents every window after any message, so the shell asks for frames during a morph and stops when it settles. A waiting card holds still.
