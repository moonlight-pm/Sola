ISLANDSSmoked glass
The material has to read as glass on a compositor that cannot blur. It does that with light, not transparency: a fill a step above the apps, a sheen that fades down, a 1 px rim where light catches the top edge, a dark edge to cut it from what is behind, and a shadow only when it floats over an app.
Layers, bottom to top
1Shadow

Two soft shadows, deep and near. Only on floats: on the band the ground is already black and a shadow does nothing.

2Edge

1 px of black at 55% outside the shape. It separates a sheet from a mail list the same gray.

3Fill

#191d26, one step above bg-secondary, so a sheet lifts off any kit surface.

4Sheen

White at 4% at the top, gone by 55% of the height. Reads as overhead light.

5Rim

1 px of white at 12% just inside the top edge, curving out along the corners.

6Content

Chrome text and marks, unchanged from Graphite.

The top-left corner at 12×, device pixels on a 1× desk

The rim lands on whole pixels. The island sits at y 6, not 6.5, so the rim is one row of device pixels. On the dogfood desk (5120 × 2160 at 1×) a half-pixel rim smears into two gray rows and the glass goes flat.

The corner gives the rim away. The rim fades as the 10 px corner turns, which is what makes it read as light on a rounded edge instead of a border.

Edge outside, rim inside. The dark edge is drawn outside the shape and the rim inside it, so the island's 28 px stay 28 px of content.

How transparent, with no blur · the same menu over Claude's transcript
Ship opaque. Turn on transparency with blur, never without it. At 86% and 92% the transcript ghosts through the menu, the same readability complaint Liquid Glass met on LCD Macs. With blur, 72% reads as glass and stays legible. River 0.4.5 does not speak ext-background-effect-v1, so v1 is the third column. When it does, the fill's alpha becomes a setting, as macOS 27 made it one.
