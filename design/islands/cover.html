What changes against Graphite

The bar becomes a band

40 px, opaque black, reserved the way the bar is. Zones start 12 px lower. The band is one surface, as the bar is today.

Islands hug their content

Three of them, 6 px from the top, 8 px from the output's edges. The ground between them is empty on purpose.

The center is live

Graphite has no center. Islands puts the one thing that is happening there: an agent, a meeting, a track, a whisper.

Surfaces grow from their island

The launcher, the switcher, and agent cards become the island. Menus, the audio panel, and the calendar hang from it.

Smoked glass

A graphite fill, a 1 px rim on top, a dark edge, and a shadow only where the glass floats over an app. Opaque until River can blur; tested on Material.

One radius family

A child's corner is its parent's minus the gap between them: card 18 → button 8, sheet 14 → row 8, island 10 → pill 7.

Kept from Graphite: flush zones without titles, one cyan signal, shape-first marks, pixel meters, the Sol gold flower, and the shadow rule (only things that float cast one).
