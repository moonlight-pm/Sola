ISLANDSSurfaces grow from their island
Every shell surface has an owner in the band, and it opens from there. Two rules cover all of them. A surface that takes over (the launcher, the switcher, an agent card) becomes its island. A surface that sits beside the work (a menu, the audio panel, the calendar) hangs 6 px under the group that opened it, and the island stays.

Become

The island is the surface. One rounded rectangle grows from the island's corner or center and shrinks back to it. Used when the surface takes the keyboard.

Hang

The group that opened it turns into a 7 px pill; a sheet hangs 6 px below, its edge aligned to the pill. Used when the work stays in view.
